# Handoff

## Include

1. Objective and current status.
2. Confirmed facts and decisions.
3. Root cause, when applicable.
4. Exact files changed.
5. Verification evidence.
6. Failed approaches and disproven hypotheses.
7. Remaining risks or incomplete items.
8. Git state: branch, uncommitted files, commits, push/deploy status.
9. Exact next action.

## Quality rule

The next agent should be able to continue without rereading the entire conversation or repeating completed checks.

Do not include hidden reasoning or speculative narrative. Preserve decisions, evidence, and operational context.
