# Evidence Handoff

## Purpose
Provide a concise, auditable completion package.

## Required sections
- objective;
- scope;
- files changed;
- behavior implemented;
- verification performed;
- evidence and results;
- known limitations;
- approvals obtained;
- remaining actions.

## Completion rule
Do not report “done” unless success criteria have been checked against observable evidence.
A successful build alone is not proof of correct business behavior.
