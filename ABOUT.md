# About Nguyễn Đăng Quang

**Nguyễn Đăng Quang** is a management-system auditor, consultant, trainer, and AI-enabled business-system designer based in Vietnam.

He graduated in Information Technology and has more than ten years of experience in auditing and consulting management systems. His professional work focuses on the practical intersection of:

- ISO/IEC 27001 information security management;
- ISO/IEC 42001 artificial intelligence management systems;
- ISO 22301 business continuity;
- ISO/IEC 20000-1 IT service management;
- risk management, governance, and audit evidence;
- digital transformation for SMEs;
- controlled use of AI agents in software and operational workflows.

## Working philosophy

Quang approaches AI-assisted development from the perspective of an auditor and system owner rather than from “vibe coding.” The central questions are:

- Is the objective clear?
- Is the agent using the correct source of truth?
- Is the implementation scope controlled?
- Can the result be demonstrated in the real environment?
- Is there enough evidence to review, roll back, and audit the change?
- Does human management remain in control of material decisions?

This perspective shaped **HQC Core**: a compact skill set for helping Codex and other coding agents work in disciplined, testable waves.

## Current direction

Quang is building and documenting practical AI operating patterns for:

- website and business-system development with coding agents;
- AI customer-service systems with human-in-the-loop supervision;
- structured decision-state views instead of hidden chain-of-thought;
- approval gates, business guardrails, and audit logs;
- reusable skills that encode project lessons and reduce repeated mistakes.
